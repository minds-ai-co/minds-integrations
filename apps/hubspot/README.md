# Minds for HubSpot

## Product workflow

Build a Minds audience from a user-selected CRM segment, create a reviewable
study plan, and attach the resulting aggregate research summary to a campaign
or company record.

## Marketplace gate

HubSpot requires a unique public app using its listing's OAuth client and at
least three active, unaffiliated production installs in the previous 30 days.
The first milestone is a consented pilot with three real customer portals, not
a public listing. Do not use test or Minds-owned portals to satisfy the gate.
